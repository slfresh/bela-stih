# Device baseline — Samsung SM-G780G, 1.5.2 (versionCode 31), 2026-09-26

The numbers every later release is measured against (plan R0; R2 haptics and
R4 sound/feel quote this file). Collected with `bash scripts/device-baseline.sh <dir>`
on the one test phone, USB, screen on, launcher in front, nothing else running.

## The phone

- Samsung Galaxy S20 FE (SM-G780G), Android 13 (API 33), build TP1A.220624.014.G780GXXSHEYJ1.
- Display modes 60 and 120 Hz; the panel runs at **120 Hz while the app is in front** and
  drops to 60 Hz idle. The PerfProbe's median-based threshold (R0) is what makes it honest at both.
- Vibrator: `mCapabilities=[AMPLITUDE_CONTROL]`, `mSupportedEffects=[]`,
  **`mSupportedPrimitives=[]`**, `mHapticChannelMaxVibrationAmplitude=0.0`. This motor has no
  composed primitives (no CLICK/TICK/THUD, no `VibrationEffect.startComposition`) and no haptic
  audio channel: R2's haptics must be amplitude-controlled waveforms with a fallback that feels
  the same, and cannot be tuned on this phone alone for phones that do have primitives.

## The app, as installed

- Permissions requested: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, RECORD_AUDIO, VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK
  (1.5.1 also asked for SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW and READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE; never used, now blocked).
- Cold start (`am start -W`, process killed each time), TotalTime to the first frame:
  **757 / 627 / 631 ms**.
- Frames over the launch and the home screen's entrance animation (gfxinfo reset before the
  third cold start, read 12 s later): 43 frames, 6 janky (14%), p50 **8 ms**, p90 14 ms,
  p95 23 ms, p99 77 ms, 2 missed vsyncs, 5 slow UI-thread frames. The home screen is static
  once settled (0 frames in 20 s idle), so a longer idle window measures nothing.
- Audio at rest on the home screen: **24 AudioFlinger tracks for the app, 0 active** (expo-audio's
  player pool; the 40-track cap is the reason clips `release()` rather than `remove()`, see
  voice notes), no AAudio MMAP stream (`AAudioClientTracker: client has 0 streams`,
  `Exclusive MMAP Endpoints: 0`), **media sessions: 0** (the expo-audio patch holds).
- Media session dump note: `dumpsys media_session` lists volume-key events by package for weeks;
  those lines are not sessions. Count `Sessions Stack - have N sessions`.

## Not measured

- **Tap-to-sound latency** (the plan's 240 fps camera method) needs a high-frame-rate camera
  pointed at the phone and a microphone; none was available. R4 has to add it before it can
  claim "≤ the R0 baseline and under 80 ms"; until then the AudioFlinger/AAudio facts above
  are the baseline for the output path (AudioTrack via the mixer, no MMAP/low-latency stream).
- Frames in play (a full deal) need scripted taps; the golden corpus replay on the phone
  (plan R1) is the intended vehicle.

Raw dumps of this run are not committed (they carry pids and a volume-key history); rerun the
script to regenerate them.
