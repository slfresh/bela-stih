Ukratko: ne pohranjujemo vaše podatke

  • Igra ne traži račun, prijavu ni e-mail. Vaš napredak (razina, novčići, statistika) sprema se samo na vašem uređaju.

  • Nema oglasa, nema praćenja, nema analitike trećih strana, nema prodaje podataka.
  • Novčići u igri su virtualni: zarađuju se isključivo igranjem, ne mogu se kupiti ni zamijeniti za pravi novac.

In short: we do not store your data

  • No account, no sign-in, no e-mail. Your progress (level, coins, statistics) is stored on your device only.
  • For online play the server receives only: the nickname you type in, your chosen avatar and your moves. That data is processed in memory for the duration of the match and is never stored.
  • As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address during a match; we do not log it or use it for anything beyond the connection itself. Technical server logs are rotated away automatically.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, no selling of data.
  • In-game coins are virtual: earned by playing only, and can never be bought or exchanged for real money.

Who is responsible, and on what basis

Data controller: Slavko (independent app developer), Croatia — contact slavkogrbic25@gmail.com. The legal basis for processing your nickname, avatar, moves and IP address during an online match is providing the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in keeping the server technically secure (Art. 6(1)(f)). Processing happens on a server in Germany (Hetzner Online GmbH, as processor); nothing leaves the EU. Retention: the duration of the match; technical logs at most a few days until automatic rotation.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Since nothing is stored, there is usually nothing to hand over or erase — but do get in touch. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority; in Croatia that is AZOP (azop.hr).

Deleting your data

You delete everything yourself: choose “Erase progress” in Settings, or simply uninstall the app. There is nothing on the server to delete.
